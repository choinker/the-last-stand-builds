At sunset the dead walk out of the eastern woods. Twenty men of the Hollow militia must hold them off until dawn: firing in volleys, mending the barricades, and falling back toward the village when a line can no longer hold.

Each line is twelve 4-metre sections. The dead batter a section until it gives way, then the whole horde pours through the gap. You decide when the company falls back. Leave too soon and you give up ground; leave too late and the men are overrun.

One night, seven waves

18:45 sunsetmidnight05:45 dawn

Walkers come first, then runners, then great hulking brutes that tear through a fence in seconds. Last until the sun rises behind the eastern woods and the dead burn in its light.

Standing orders

  • Fire when the sergeant calls Present! or Fire! and your shot hits half again as hard.
  • Aim for the head. A ball to the skull drops a walker outright.
  • A broken section can be rebuilt. Hold E until it stands again.
  • If you fall, you take up the musket of the nearest man still standing.
  • Lose if ten of the dead reach the houses, or when the last man falls.
